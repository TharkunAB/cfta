# Actual polynomial selection and distinct ranks

`MicroCFTA/LatticeSelect.lean` connects the source formula compiler, stored elimination levels, cumulative polynomials, binary selection, residual ranks, and point closure. It imports `FullSyntax`, `LatticeAPI`, `LatticeCumulative`, and `SearchCost`.

`compiled_coordinate_table` derives a finite section table from the finite-support witness of the actual next stored level. The table is a proof witness. The decoder does not construct or enumerate it. The theorem proves equality between its natural counts and the actual rational polynomial count, cumulative values, and values at each coordinate. The initial zero-or-one indicator comes from successful core compilation. `cumulative_natural` and `cumulative_monotone` therefore derive integrality, nonnegativity, and monotonicity. They do not take these properties as assumptions.

`sourceBounds_exists` proves that a live prefix makes the source's partial `minimum` and `maximum` calls safe. The bounds come from the actual generated cumulative intervals. `cumulative_before` and `cumulative_after` establish zero below the lower bound and the complete parent count at the upper bound. `select_exists` uses the source rational comparison and exact numerator subtraction. It produces a live next coordinate and a valid residual rank. `select_encode` proves the inverse cumulative-offset step. `select_query_bound` applies `SearchCost.logarithmic_queries` to this polynomial cumulative predicate. The bound counts cumulative queries. It does not bound polynomial compilation, polynomial evaluation, or arbitrary-precision arithmetic.

`decodeFrom` iterates the source coordinate search. `rankFrom` sums its cumulative offsets and checks final formula membership. `decodeFrom_exists`, `rankFrom_range`, and `decodeFrom_rankFrom` prove existence, valid rank bounds, and inverse decoding. `ranked_iff_admits` connects this ranker to the independently evaluated formula and the required tuple length. `root_count` connects the current-prefix count to the actual stored polynomial constant sum.

`points_exact` proves that the actual polynomial decoder maps `Fin n` bijectively to all satisfying integer tuples. `fullSyntax_points_exact` derives the same result for every successful parse of the current complete formula syntax. `points_api_exact` starts from successful public point construction and includes its stored integer count and dimension. `compiledPoints_exact` discharges the exact-points requirement of `ZeroSize.Points`. `closePoints_exact` then proves that closing generator outcomes over the actual compiled integer points neither removes nor inserts formula solutions.

Successful parsing and successful level construction are input conditions. They identify the source's supported finite fragment. Count correctness, positive support, cumulative monotonicity, exact coverage, and rank injectivity are conclusions. Unsupported or unbounded formulas remain explicit source errors. The proof uses unbounded natural dimensions and ranks. Machine-sized dimensions, Haskell map representation, compiled Haskell equivalence, and solver implementation remain external boundaries.

Validation: `lake build MicroCFTA.LatticeSelect` succeeds with the pinned Lean toolchain. Kernel audits of `points_api_exact`, `compiledPoints_exact`, `closePoints_exact`, and the supporting correspondence theorems report only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. There is no `sorryAx` or project-specific axiom.
