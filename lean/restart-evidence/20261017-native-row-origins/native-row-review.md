construct general native completion. Each child call belongs to the same ordered
walk. Its prefix, child, and suffix counts sum to that walk count. Its initial and
returned states include the actual equality-operation cache and native tables.

fixed source universe for later adaptive queries. Initial `Original` hypotheses
