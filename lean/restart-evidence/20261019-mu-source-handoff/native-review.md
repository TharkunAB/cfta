`714f0c3666b0482b29da840ea45d07ce374b4e8772507b4e0bda32d2f5f52ea5`.
It has three public heads: `Call`, `union`, and `reference`.

`Call` retains the exact child request interval, Good at both ends,

returned-cell retention, and the full row Origin at that same reader.
The producers derive both input codecs and the request key from the actual
successful call and its Origin. A direct unkeyed successful call returns

an exact returned cell and its earlier captured writer. No internal-entry
history is assumed.

`union` starts with a literal returned parent cell and its actual union
branch. Causal history recovers one captured protected compiler interval.
The proof inverts that same interval into its ordered member walk and
constructor suffix. The complete output Origin selects an exact member
index, its result index, and the prefix, child and suffix traces. Actual
effects and returned-cell retention carry the child cell into the reader.
The member has strictly smaller structural mass than the selected union
operand. This is a source-member bound, not a bound on the generated
result or on arbitrary repeated native calls.

`reference` recovers the captured reference writer, the interned name in
its entered domain, and the exact binding read at the unchanged checked
entry. The child call preserves operand order. Its complete Origin derives
a keyed returned cell through `Call`. No mass decrease is asserted for

Neither endpoint requires closed inputs, Original syntax, physical

law. The Origin remains at the original reader. The proof does not move
it to an allocation heap. The existing self-branch identity theorem is
reused as prior coverage; no duplicate self theorem is added.
