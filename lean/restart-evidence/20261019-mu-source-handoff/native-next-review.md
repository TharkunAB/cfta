# Exact remaining source handoff

The new union theorem transports the same complete row to an actual
indexed member request. Its strict bound concerns that original member
and its union operand. Reference resolution transports the row through
the actual entered-domain read. It can resolve to an entire Mu. A smaller
reference syntax value therefore does not imply a smaller resolved value.

The existing ordinary positive-row endpoint retains actual joined input
rows, their full guard conjunction and ordered paired child requests.
It derives a positive source side and smaller original source children.
That result and the new branch handoffs cover distinct local transitions.
They do not by themselves identify an initial source occurrence after
arbitrary Mu reconstruction and native intersection.

In particular, `NativeMuWriterSourceRows.Anchor` gives a syntactic `RowAt`
inside the generated body. Its path can cross a nested Mu. `RowAt` does
not imply a literal physical row or an `Origin` at a selected reader.
The next actual producer must either expose a literal row or retain the
real nested-Mu unfolding and the fixed-reader Origin tail. It must keep
the actual stored winner and current callback distinct. It cannot align
independent existential writer intervals by assertion.

Positive full guards can be introduced by an actual conjunction. General
operand ancestry must retain that paired guard derivation and the ordered
child requests. It cannot require the unchanged full guard of one original
operand. An accessor equality can also disappear when a joined guard is
outside the positive parser. Existing parser boundaries remain applicable.

No renewal bound, exclusion of active owner collisions, unrestricted
recursive completion, convergence theorem, or Haskell correspondence is
proved by this component. No Haskell bytes change or new defect is claimed.
