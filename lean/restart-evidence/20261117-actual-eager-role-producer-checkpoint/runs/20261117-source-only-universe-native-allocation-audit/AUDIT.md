## Answer and exact predicate boundary

The original accepted source heap has no Mu or exactly one FULL stored U. This is
proved by CurrentUniverseOwnership.current_build_single_universe (286–310). Its
current_stored_universe_rows (332–358) and current_build_shallow (406–417) identify
that full U and its depth-zero body. StartupAtoms.closed_startup_value_atoms
(66–97) derives the only-U boundary property for each closed available source value.
No arbitrary unproductive Mu is substituted for the original source U.

Two properties must remain distinct:

1. Heap inventory: every complete `.mu` in nodeRecords is the same full source U.
2. Value Profile: closed value plus every atom reached through ordinary children
   or union members equals `.mu uid`. Atoms stops at a Mu (BoundaryAtoms 21–37).

Profile does not itself assert anything about unrelated records in the heap.
Conversely, a full heap-inventory fact plus Availability constrains resident Mu
occurrences even in a ready memo payload. ClosedMuClassification17–51 identifies
a closed available Mu with the FULL original U from its source atom, Facts and
source growth. Identity alone is used with actual coherence, not arbitrary handles.
