and every actual Some stopped candidate. It claims no cache for unhandled.

the captured input, L=scs.length, A the original arity ceiling, and T the original
projection-width ceiling. Duplicate buckets remain counted by the actual mass.

of addition and multiplication. It adds no root, safety, future cache or native
