# Remaining recursive progress obligations

Active-key exclusion and finite completion are separate obligations.
Excluding a busy memo key does not bound a chain of fresh recursive
requests. A full progress proof must give a finite bound or a well-founded
measure. Parent reduction can construct new values. The finite input graph
alone does not give a bound on all later constructed values.

The current operation-growth result retains earlier allocations. It also
transports structural interner validity from an initially valid cache.
It does not establish Ready for every new payload or validity of every new
completed operation entry. Initial semantic and field invariants must
still reach each reader.
The actual ancestry result identifies each evaluating owner. It does not
exclude a collision with that owner.

The raw stored-class result derives a local nonempty child obligation or
contradiction. It separates that obligation from a ready Top owner key.
New guard-only classes and later class-edit phases remain separate.
Crossing classes can disappear at both immediate children. Non-Top owners
need a more general causal argument.

Repeated-pass convergence is a further obligation. Finite completion of
one pass does not imply that repeated passes reach a fixed point.
