# Actual operation growth through outer ancestry

The three modules derive operation growth from literal calls. They do not use
completed graph certificates, ranges, semantic callbacks, or a desired growth
premise. The lower worker proof includes native instructions, conjunction,
Merge, projection, Target, Force, Leaf, edits, class passes, seeded queries,
Scan, Required, Local, and Parent. Stopped outcomes also retain old entries.
The outer proof covers node calls and bodies, rows, descendants, children,
and indexed walks. Terminal Mu and union branches use their actual code.

`phase` derives growth for every bounded outer outcome. `step` uses only the
literal branch equations and completed earlier calls already present in
`OuterExecutionAncestry.Step`. `reached` composes these derived facts.
`GrowingAncestor` retains the exact activation request, selected handle,
captured owner, evaluating cell, and unfinished body-to-reader prefix. Its
last field is derived operation growth from that activation to the reader.
`initial` starts from an actual node-busy failure at an initially empty outer
result table. It constructs the dispatch and a growing ancestor for every
current evaluating handle. It does not take a supplied prefix or graph.

Growth is unconditional. Result correctness and complete operation-memo
validity are not consequences of growth on malformed caches or inputs.
`cache_valid` preserves initial validity of the constraint cache through its
actual allocation steps. `ready` and `field` retain an initial ready value
and a successful exact field load. `identity` combines this cache validity
and retained readiness with canonical identity uniqueness. These are local
consequences of the derived prefix, not per-prefix assumptions.

The first constructed check builds its source heap. It starts a real owner
and executes a busy indexed walk at that preactivated caller. It proves the
exact busy state, active owner, full key, ready value, field, and growth.
It is not a cold-root busy counterexample. The second check constructs a
nonempty equality field, starts an indexed walk, executes its actual Empty
child call, and returns successfully. The constraint counter strictly grows.
The complete original field and ready value persist. Both public witness
statements are exact aliases of private checked proofs for finite printing.
No future graph, output law, or completed-prefix hypothesis is a fixture
input. Their private source setup copies the earlier frozen outer fixture;
that fixture remains unchanged.

Selected strict logs are operations-direct-4, workers-direct-9,
outer-direct-5, and kernels-2. Each has zero bytes. Canonical operation1,
worker2, and outer1 builds pass. They replay only the unchanged older
InternedFreshOrdinaryClosedCompletion unused-variable warning. All earlier
attempts remain present. In particular, worker-direct-8 exited zero but has
a 287-byte `omit` warning. It is not selected as an empty strict check.
The declaration was moved outside the Symbol instance section. The first
kernel attempt failed at a syntactic rewrite and reported an unused simp
argument. The second attempt uses an explicit literal child-call equation.

The public group checks all 21 handwritten declarations. The constructed
group checks two witnesses. Each group includes all eight explicit print
options, full signatures, proof printing, and axiom reports. All selected
axioms are within propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. Each worker
records effective memory.max and memory.high of 4294967296 bytes and
memory.swap.max of zero. The verifier rejects printer omissions and printer
failures. It checks the exact immutable executed sources and raw logs.

This result does not exclude physical key collision with an active owner.
It also does not bound the number of fresh recursive requests. These are
separate obligations. Parent can construct new values, so an eventual
no-busy theorem alone would not prove general outer termination. The work
does not change Haskell or establish full runtime correspondence.
