Thus the row's SourceRow cannot come from an independently chosen callback. Rows can be nullary or occur structurally below a retained Mu.

## Actual unfolded rows

It does not apply a new writer selector or change the event when the observed row changes. No uniqueness of all possible writer proofs or publication events is asserted.

unfold_rows executes the stored BODY under the exact self-only replacement map. It derives body availability and replacements from the available full Mu. StoredLiteralCalls.execute derives semantic, heap-growth, issued-growth and output-availability facts for the actual return. A rich Context for that same encoded body build yields its actual physical publication History segment. The proof rewrites the exact successful outcome and composes this segment with the supplied initial physical history. It uses the actual return heap as execute_row's reader with reflexive growth. It requires no growth backwards to the old reader and no future enclosing completion.

The selected literal row then follows execute_row and source_row. The map's only possible replacement is the exact full self Mu, and LiteralRootParts.Row has no Mu constructor. The replacement-origin exclusion is derived by constructor inversion. The output Anchor retains an actual stored RowAt original, equal symbol and guard, equal arity, and either exact row identity or actual ordered lexical Calls. A child lookup is not required. Therefore nullary rows are covered. Rebuilt child values are not asserted equal to their original children.

## Limits

The captured publication is rooted in its actual generated encode program and captured leaf pool. These results do not identify that root with the original user program or establish original lexical binding ancestry. A stored RowAt may be structural beneath a retained Mu; that fact alone is not an observed row. UnfoldRows handles literal rows reached through root unions of ONE actual successful unfold at the fixed current heap. It does not assert that an unfold terminates, nor completeness for an arbitrary residual chain of further Mu observations. An arbitrary external graph view does not receive a fabricated publication History.

The leaf index is an occurrence in leaves. No encode.slot identity, whole-leaf/child-result equality, strict generated-value size decrease, global renewal bound, termination or Haskell refinement is claimed. Initial full-cell provenance remains a separate source-scope problem. Existing callback and nested cache-winner alternatives remain explicit.

The earlier CapturedMuRowProduction and ActiveMuBodyOrigins foundations were authored by this reviewer. The three new consumers were reviewed independently. The earlier design note and its fifteen dependency pins are copied unchanged and remain design evidence; their proposed adapters are not counted as additional checked declarations.
