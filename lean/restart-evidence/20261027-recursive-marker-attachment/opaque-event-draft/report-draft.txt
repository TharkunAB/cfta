Native and cache boundaries
The own-prefix, child and finalizer observations use the supplied fixed bounds.
No native operation is rerun. ChildTraversal.complete_trace observes its SAME
actual completed call. A nonterminal child preserves its native endpoint, but
later suffix calls may change Caller and queries; those actual endpoints remain
in the suffix Trace and finalizer equation. Full Memos equality is not inferred
from a query projection. Finalization uses the reached native heap.

The pure allocation equation does not prove Safe, alignment or IsolatedPending
for an arbitrary malformed input cache. In particular counter need not equal
values.length without genuine input invariants. No error, missing-cache, raw[]
rejection, unsupported preparation or stopped traversal is promoted to completion.
The observer is conditional on an ACTUAL completed receipt. It does not produce
that receipt from a desired future success premise.
