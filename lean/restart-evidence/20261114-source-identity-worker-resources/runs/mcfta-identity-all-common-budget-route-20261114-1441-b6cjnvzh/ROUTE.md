Why eventual completion is insufficient

runEager_identity_finite_complete_resources gives one result above a threshold.
It says nothing about a smaller workFuel result unless an actual execution law
connects them. A stopped scan can retain earlier outputs. Even if the final
small-budget native endpoint is invalid or unavailable, those earlier outputs
still require their own packets. Returning True for every stopped worker would
lose precisely these outputs.

Checked prefix gate and its exact limitations

ObservedPrefixFuel.prefix_identity_resources_at_any_budget produces actual
CurrentPrefixResources at each supported identity prefix. Its exemptions are:
.stopped; .parked with no candidate adapter; and .narrowed with .failed native
preparation. The checked unsupported_prefix_stops consumer is PRIVATE in that
module. Its actual unfolding proves stopped/missing-adapter prefixes stop the
positive-depth worker with [] outputs and the original endpoint. A new module
must expose that tiny law or repeat its exact simplification; it cannot call
that private name as a public API. Actual failed-prefix shapes also need their
producer equation or exclusion proof. The resource theorem deliberately gives
no fields for its failed exemption.

CurrentPrefixResources contains a parked Packet, and the continuing Right
shape contains Ready, stored bucket and Packet. It does NOT contain parked
mass, nonempty exact mass, bucket length relative to the original mass, or
IncomingAvoid. The eventual identity_checkpoint_resources supplies these at a
large common bound. To use existing prepared resource kernels at an arbitrary
supported prefix, first recover these fields from the actual read/check frames,
or expose the checked private supported-prefix replay argument and transport
one exact larger checkpoint. This is a missing pointwise phase wrapper, not
permission to assume future phase fields. Parked mass uses PrefixMass.parked_mass;
false check preserves values; original bucket length comes from the actual
stored original bucket; incoming avoidance uses the actual read/check transport.

Recommended execution route

1. Prove successful_run_moreFuel for actual SelectedPreparation.run. This is the
   first new native execution producer after the prefix gate. Entry success
   replays the getValue and representative successes, including the exact
   compressed core and ancestry write. SelectedValue.run_done_moreFuel replays
   the exact native value and heap. One small candidate for that native lemma

   Compare the exact replayed observation against complete_resources_current
   above its sufficient bound. This transfers the complete current Packet,
   Post, Fits, Heap, mass, Ready and IncomingAvoid to the actual small-budget
   successful preparation. A failed/malformed preparation emits no output.

2. Prove continuable_child_run_moreFuel for actual ChildDispatch.run, including
   raw [] rejections and singleton successes with a cache. The exact reached
   caller, query memos, cache, outer core and waiting map must replay together.
   SourceEqualityNativeDemand.done_stable and native call_done replay are
   existing ingredients. NativeIndex.write_some_moreFuel covers successful
   cache writes. SharedChildResources.complete_with_fragments_resources is
   eventual; it is not itself a pointwise child replay law. Its successful
   observation can supply all fields after exact replay identifies endpoints.
   Replaying only a fragment or only a singleton result is insufficient: an
   empty rejection can advance native globals and then start a later sibling.

3. At scan, prove actual output origin or prefix replay by induction on edges.
   For head .stopped processed outputs current, every retained output is from
   that same stopped visitor. For head .complete processed outputs reached
   saved, the tail starts at exactly reached/saved. The combined complete OR
   stopped tail emits outputs ++ remaining. List.mem_append separates the head
   and tail cases. Record the completed row prefix and its actual endpoint;
   do not quantify a hypothetical future visitor law as a final theorem input.
   visitChildren places the tail inside each successful child continuation.
   Its proof must therefore follow each reached callback, not only a completed
   child traversal list. Ordinary height decreases along actual prepared
   children. The terminal/opaque branch uses the continuable child law.

4. Empty catalogs already have the public
   IdentityResultMetadataErasure.continue_empty_exact law. It gives the exact
   original Core, updated types, actual caller/queries, and restart. It needs
   no fresh callback replay family. Finalize.run_some_moreFuel is also public
   and already replays the entire successful cache. Failed finalization has
   no new outputs in that branch; earlier scan outputs still remain.

5. Use completed-result replay plus stopped-output inclusion as the induction
   invariant. REPLAY-TARGET states the proposed internal law. At fixed depth,
   every small .complete result replays exactly at all sufficiently larger
   bounds. Every small .stopped result's emitted outputs occur in such larger
   results; its reason and optional endpoint need not be equal. A stopped hook
   keeps its recursive partial outputs through ReceiptContinuation.stopped_hook
   and finish. A stopped row suffix retains completed-prefix outputs through
   SharedBranching.scan. This invariant lets an actual completed native prefix
   supply the next sibling's Context without demanding failure-endpoint safety.

6. Combine that proved replay law with the checked eventual worker theorem.
   If B is the eventual stable threshold and L is the replay lift for workFuel f,
   take the SAME larger budget f + L + B. Replay uses extra B. Eventual completion
   uses extra f + L, so addition commutativity identifies the observations.
   Every small output is then literally an output of the known resource result.
   Its OutputResources and Packet.pending/ordered transfer directly. No unsafe
   inference from mere existence of a larger completion is made.

Actual worker cases that must be retained

- Recursive depth zero: none/done emit their literal original output; stuck and
  expand emit no output, with expand carrying the actual exhausted receipt.
- Positive depth none/done/stuck: the same literal behavior.
- Selected prefix stopped or missing parked adapter: stopped [] at entry.
- Supported parked []: complete [] after blocked restart.
- Supported parked singleton: smaller recursive depth at the exact framed cache.
  Its result may be stopped with nonempty partial outputs.
- Empty/rejecting Right phase: complete [] at its actual reached caller.
- Continuing Right phase: actual successful preparation, then ordinary scan or
  opaque receipt continuation. Unsupported preparation stops with [] locally.
- Captured edge/fallback unsupported/finalize-stopped: [] locally. Previous row
  outputs remain in the enclosing stopped result.
- Completed receipt plus stopped recursive hook: preserve ALL hook outputs and
  the exact continuation receipt. No blanket stopped exemption is allowed.
- Completed rows followed by stopped row/tail: preserve prefix outputs and the
  stopped row's own outputs in order. Membership suffices for this resource
  target; exact stream order/multiplicity remains a separate original clause.
