A direct acyclic path graph does not prove joint compatibility.
Consider removed requirements A.[1]=B, A.[1]=C and B.[1]=C.
The first two requirements force B=C. The third then requires B.[1]=B.
No finite term can contain itself as a proper subterm.
A proof must retain same-path congruences and the actual UF merges and branch ancestry.
This is a logical design caution, not a reachable source counterexample or a Haskell defect.
Independent witnesses for individual buckets do not prove one witness for the combined history.
