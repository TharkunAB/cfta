## Two different heap predicates

`RequiredHeapEffects` uses `SourceEqualityAnnotationHeap.Heap property`: every physical EDGE key satisfies a predicate. `CurrentNative.advance` requires `ConstrainedCapGuardHeap.Heap Plain`: each completed Mu body and its stored ordinary probe have equal guard profiles. These predicates are different. Do not pass the former as the latter or assume every physical edge guard is Plain. [R3, R11, R12]

The adapter can use the receipt's `required.ready.semantic` and `required.ready.costs` directly. Its correct guard producer is public `RequiredGuard.children`, not an all-edges-Plain instantiation of `RequiredHeapEffects`. The latter may be used with a trivial predicate for other physical effects if a proof needs them, but it is not needed for the proposed minimal route.
