## Terminal cases and boundaries

Empty, union, reference and Mu bodies have no outgoing Step in this literal graph. Even though some worker bodies can return immediately or fail, they cannot witness the supplied body-to-node `inside`. A zero-fuel ordinary body has no `body_rows` Step. These cases close by the graph inversion, without a closure, plainness, exposure or future-completion premise. If the immediate selected child has a nonordinary value, the direct alternative still uses the general one-hop rank; its activation alternative cannot have a further body-to-node prefix unless the captured body is ordinary. The induction handles this same terminal inversion at each hop.

This theorem would compare actual requests across an unfinished enclosing body. It is not every cache contract. To exclude ALL busy cells, a separate consumer must show that the cell came from an enclosing activation. Public `Reached.owners`477–495 requires NodeValid and an initially NodeIdle table. Public `OuterExecutionAncestry.initial`695–720 derives that ancestry from the actual initial empty outer tables and a literal busy failure. Without initial idleness, a preexisting evaluating cell need not have an activation inside the chosen prefix. The proposed body theorem adds no empty-table or NodeIdle premise because it starts from an explicit actual body prefix. Equality of a later busy request with an enclosing full request, other failures, and full completion remain separate consumers.
