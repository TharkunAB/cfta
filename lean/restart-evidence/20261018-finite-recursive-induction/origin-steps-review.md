# Actual epsilon step induction

The final production source has 14 public declarations. Path counts actual front epsilon steps at one fixed reader. RowAt ends at a complete physical row. Origin and bare-reference observations both have such finite witnesses. An ordinary row witness has count zero. Each union or Mu witness has positive count and a tail with one fewer step. A Mu witness derives its literal successful stored unfolding. A valid reader also gives the actual execute equation, returned validity and Grows. The tail keeps the original reader.

The row and reference induction laws pass each shorter actual witness and its induction hypothesis to the caller. They do not assume Mu completion. They derive it from an observed nonempty successor. Counts concern one existing epsilon observation. They do not bound all recursive execution, make generated values available at the original reader, or prove reader transport. No binder equivalence or source correspondence law is assumed.

Selected strict origin-steps-direct-3 has a personally inspected zero-byte log. Canonical origin-steps-canonical-1 passes 546 jobs without warnings. Full origin-steps-public-1 prints all 14 complete statements, proofs and axiom reports. Hardened checks pass all eight options, no omissions and only the three standard axioms. Every job uses pinned Lean 4.34.1, 4 GiB maximum and high limits, zero swap and a five-minute time cap.
