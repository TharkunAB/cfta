## Carrier meaning and actual primitive operations

The four existing predicates retain their meaning. GraphValue denotes a
stored graph restriction or enumerated fragment. CarriesGraph says that an
equality class contains such a value. FragmentCarries applies this condition
to every fragment hole. ReferencesCarry applies it to stored enumerated
fragments and retained residuals. No new predicate or evaluator is introduced.

CarrierFrame derives carrier and reference transport from the actual full
compression Frame. TerminalPrefixCarriers follows the actual representative
scan and class unions. It derives the final Total forest and SameClass links
for recorded sources. AssimilationCarriers follows the actual source and
winner reads. It uses the real graph-free merge result and removes the source
slot only after transferring its graph carrier to the related winner.

IncomingCarriers follows the actual incoming write. The winner stores SOME
native handle. The proof establishes the target carrier without requiring the
target argument to be its own representative. Cached adapters use the actual
raw result and adaptation equation. The SOME handle zero used for an Empty
merge supplies structural provenance; this theorem does not claim acceptance
or Safe for a rejected candidate.

FoldCarriers composes actual dispatch steps. It retains forests, old carriers
and stored references across successful, rejected and stopped outcomes.
OwnFinalizeCarriers covers fresh own-constraint allocation and finalization.
Fresh free variables with NONE handles are not claimed to carry graphs.
Finalization consumes actual FragmentCarries and retains all old carriers.

TerminalCarriers combines prefix union, cached fold and incoming writes.
It covers the actual outer state, including faults, stops and rejection,
as well as successful returned fragments and attached caches. ChildCarriers
uses the actual shallow or terminal branch. Shallow preparation retains a
SOME handle for ordinary, Mu and Union children, including empty buckets.
Successful returned fragments therefore have graph-bearing holes.

## Resource producers and recursive histories

TraversalResourcesCarriers retains the entire old 4287-byte declaration after
theorem rename and grouping. Its added observer follows the full old result.
The original head producer runs once in each recursive step. Its actual
successful result supplies Safe for the next suffix. The proof does not assume
that raw trace existence implies Safe. Returned fragments keep carriers even
when a later traversal step stops.

SelectedEdgeResourcesCarriers and CapturedEdgeResourcesCarriers retain the
entire old 4184-byte and 4422-byte declarations in the same way. Own allocation,
actual child traversal, residual recording and captured finalization share the
same cache and fragment. Their added reference observer supplies no new input
gate. Stopped-prefix fragments and the full original resource packets remain.

SelectedPrefixCarriers and PrefixCarriers follow actual repeated-variable
checks and narrowing actions. They keep the original PrefixValid contract.
CallbackCarriers follows actual recordTypes success and its compression frame
for either Boolean result. It assumes no callback non-rejection.

HeadReceiptCarriers uses the actual CompletedHeadReceipt and its original
Packet, reservation and projection fields. It obtains the enriched captured
producer once, then compares the two real executions at a common sufficient
bound. The complete result identifies the SAME cache, caller and fragment.
No relation between the current work fuel and the new bound is assumed.

HistoryReceiptCarriers adds actual entry reads and callback recording.
FiniteHeadCarriers retains the entire previous 4589-byte head declaration.
It invokes the original with-receipt producer once. A carrier observer follows
the complete previous result. It covers an actual completed head receipt;
it does not assume properties of arbitrary future callback outputs.

ResourceHistoryCarriers proves carrier preservation to the SAME actual
emitted output. It follows each history constructor. Done and stopped use
the retained cache. Parked follows the actual restart cache. Ordinary follows
the actual prefix, entry, head receipt, callback and recursive tail. The
original HistoryAdmission.Packet supplies Total. ReferencesCarry is required
only at the original history input, never at a future endpoint.

OutputRenderOrigin retains the complete previous output-render return. It
moves root and reference carrier conditions from the output to the ORIGINAL
history input. The history derives the output conditions. Output Discharged
remains explicit. InitialRenderCarriers removes even the input carrier gates
by deriving them from literal startup. Its complete old 4801-byte declaration
recovers exactly after theorem rename, grouping and removal of the appended
observer. The old producer runs once. No original contract field is removed.
