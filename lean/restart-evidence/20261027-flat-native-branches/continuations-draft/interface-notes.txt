Genuine old/flat relations

HookResultRelated retains literal output list/order, final Caller/query in the complete arm, and literal reason/Option endpoint in the stopped arm. OptionalReceiptRelated preserves absent-versus-present capture and relates the complete selected receipt. StopRelated distinguishes edge/continuation stops, retains the actual current receipt, and equates the stop reason and endpoint. ResultRelated uses ordered All₂ SelectedEdge.ResultRelated for EVERY processed receipt; all output values/order are literal. Complete final Caller/query are literal. Stopped outputs retain exact StopRelated. SelectedEdge.ResultRelated includes full child raw diagnostics, outer-core relation, optional-cache relation and native effects; no raw list or error position is omitted.

No all-hook completion or callback-language/resource theorem is claimed. HookResult does not carry a new cache. A hook may use its captured cache internally; its output endpoint alone threads into the next sibling. No Safe, native-index validity, whole recursive completion or final assignment follows from these relations alone. An absent stop endpoint remains absent.

Conditional transport boundary

A future run_related proof must have BOTH explicit compatibility of the actual hook results on related caches AND actual captured-edge result relations at each Caller/query reached along THIS hook-aware run. A legacy branch trace alone is insufficient after a hook changes Caller/query. Positive captured completion alone does not justify matching none/error observations at arbitrary inadequate old representative/index fuels. The all-input identity embedding avoids this gap by comparing two actual FLAT workers at identical native bounds. This draft therefore does not add an unproved universal captured map or assume that a supplied recursive hook completes.
