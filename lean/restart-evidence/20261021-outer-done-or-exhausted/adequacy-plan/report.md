Existing local numeric producers already supply the one-Parent growth image. No additional Parent wrapper is justified.
The genuine missing design is a concrete controlled lexicographic work/seed envelope. It remains undefined and unproved; this survey constructs no fuel F and proposes no public completion header.

The initial rank is exactly PositiveQuotientCount.count on the initial inherited payload and value, paired with literal value mass. Count is finite; strict descent alone is not a bound on the number of calls.
A count drop permits mass growth. The numeric envelope therefore needs the evolving profile and guard alphabet as additional data. Its recursive calls still decrease the rank, not that seed.

The concrete recurrence must account for sequential sibling work and the exact body/rows/walk fuel decrements. A maximum activation depth is insufficient.
