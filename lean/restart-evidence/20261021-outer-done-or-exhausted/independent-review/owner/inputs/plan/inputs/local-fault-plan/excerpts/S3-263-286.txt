  273       Post formulas solver next (caller first) (caller last) final ∧

  275       Carrier (fun guard => guard ∈ current.1) (caller last).checked.native.machine.heap ∧ Inputs last ∧
  276       Le (forecast budget last current).2 (forecast budget first seed).2 ∧
  277       (forecast budget last current).1 ⊆ (forecast budget first seed).1 ∧
  278       Room bits (forecast budget last current).2 ∧
  279       Fits (forecast budget first seed).2 (caller last).checked ∧
