The count boundary is intentional. Arbitrarily insufficient old countFuel can return none while direct flat counts succeed. Therefore prepareChild_completed is forward transport of actual old success, not an all-bound optional-cache equivalence. A consumer should invoke the original adequate producer once and retain its full Safe/storage/realization/native-context packet, then apply this theorem at its actual successful bound. None of these representation relations derives Safe, satisfiability, fragment acceptance, or native Context by itself. Runtime Caller/query work is outside these pure own/shallow stages.
