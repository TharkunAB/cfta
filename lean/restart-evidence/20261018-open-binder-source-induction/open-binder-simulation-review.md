## Why the previous sufficient condition is too strong

SourceCurrentOpenWeakSimulation.Forward is sound. Its same theorem requires both Forward reader P and Forward reader (fun right left => P left right). Consequently every pair in P must have the same observations. This also applies to pairs introduced after an epsilon step.

Actual union normalization can group ordinary rows. Let A and B be different nullary ordinary nodes. Let AB be the actual makeUnion [A,B] result. Let z be a bare reference. The grouped union makeUnion [AB,z] can expose AB or z, but cannot expose A alone. A flat mixed union makeUnion [A,B,z] retains A and B separately, because z blocks the all-ordinary fast path. Both complete roots can have the same literal atoms and full rows.

The left step to A therefore need not have one observation-equivalent reached value on the right. A has fewer rows than AB. The complete grouped union also exposes z. This rules out using the previous two-way sufficient condition as a complete normalization congruence method. It does not refute that theorem or observational equality.

A separate constructed component uses actual raw unused Mu wrappers around A and B, a bare z, and the exact grouped makeUnion schedule. Its selected kernels-5 and full public-1 prove both the old-condition obstruction and a new directional instance. Its manifest has 39 entries and SHA 482542fe84ce68625bab3a0d464d22f8a4d704e3be792ac4ed102be00da3cac5. This module's proof does not depend on that fixture or on an asserted output equivalence.

## New checked local condition

Mutual P first second means P first second and P second first. Directed reader P requires three local facts for each P pair. It preserves bare references in one direction. A physical row matches a complete right origin with the same full symbol and guard and ordered Mutual P children. Each left epsilon successor matches a reached right endpoint under P in one direction.

The same theorem needs only one Directed law and Mutual membership of the two compared roots. It does not require a global law for the converse relation. Its depth induction applies mutual membership only to paired row children. Its finite path proof keeps the direction of P unchanged. This permits A P AB after a union step without requiring AB P A.

No reflexivity or transitivity premise is needed for this sufficient condition. The name denotes the intended directional use of P; the module does not assert that an arbitrary P is a preorder. The related theorem adds explicit physical Empty agreement at the compared roots. Same alone does not supply that agreement. The observation reader remains fixed throughout all paths and row observations.

The proof reuses the previous one-way path and reference theorems after forgetting the reverse child membership. The row theorem retains both child directions when it transfers a complete origin. The reverse root comparison applies the same Directed law to the second member of Mutual. It does not invoke a converse simulation.

## Precise remaining producer

A useful next source relation must be defined from actual calls, rather than from Same at the desired depth. A candidate P can be generated by exact paired raw-source and stored-execution endpoints, identity, composition, and normalization fragments. Resolved lexical slots and ordered constructor children need physical Empty agreement in addition to mutual P membership. General epsilon tails must not require that physical Empty agreement: a nonempty Mu can unfold to Empty.

A current binder result pair can then provide its own P self-slot membership by an actual-call generator. The local Directed proof would construct P membership for its successors. It would not assume Same at the same depth or recursively invoke Directed for a newly generated successor pair. Each chosen older writer would use its own expression and exact shared sparse maps to connect its stored body with its ordinary probe. The current ordinary probes would then connect the two source calls. This is a proposed proof architecture, not a checked source instance.

Two gaps remain in that construction. First, the actual-call generators must be closed under root filtering, smart cleanup, and the selected earlier-writer readback while retaining all literal alternatives. Existing RootMatch constructors and bare-reference reflection cover portions of this step. A general open Mu atom still needs an actual successor-membership producer.

Second, the relation must live at a supported observation reader. Actual calls at another execution heap do not imply support in an arbitrary view. ObservationReaderSupport.prepare constructs a new supported reader for final closed available values. It does not prove that the original cold input relation transports to that reader. Automatic support for arbitrary open captures remains unproved. No arbitrary-view source instance is asserted here.

Publication History contains real callback subtrees. An earlier selected writer can be located before a publication. However Written and Writer erase an explicit proper-subtree index, and actual later reconstruction may publish new records after that old reader. Therefore chronological evidence alone is not a proved globally decreasing tuple. A finite epsilon witness decreases only along the selected left observation path. The new directional law uses that valid decrease without claiming a source, publication, or termination rank.

The remaining general theorem must supply these actual source and reader facts. Replacing them with the desired whole-parent RootMatch, a same-depth Related self hypothesis, or a future successful call would not close the gap. The current results establish no general recursive completion, all-active-owner exclusion, or complete public Haskell correspondence. No Haskell defect or patch is claimed.
