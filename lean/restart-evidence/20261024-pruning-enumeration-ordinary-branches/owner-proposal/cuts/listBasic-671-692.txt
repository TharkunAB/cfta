/-! ### singleton -/

/--
Constructs a single-element list.

Examples:
 * `List.singleton 5 = [5]`.
 * `List.singleton "green" = ["green"]`.
 * `List.singleton [1, 2, 3] = [[1, 2, 3]]`
-/
@[inline] protected def singleton {α : Type u} (a : α) : List α := [a]
