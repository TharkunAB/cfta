# Actual writer reconstruction and semantic progress review

Checked on 2026-10-18. This review uses the source bytes pinned below. It makes no new all-depth source claim.

The actual writer reconstruction chain is sound. `PublicationReconstructionGenerators.publication` derives both `Generated` directions from one exact publication, arbitrary common outer `Replacements`, and one arbitrary available local replacement. Its premises retain both actual warm calls. The proof reconstructs the historical raw callbacks and adjacent filters, obtains live aliases and own-reference tests from actual freshness, and replays the raw builds and filters. Shared sparse maps agree with both original executions. The proof does not assume `Same`, `Directed`, or `BodyIH`.

`StoredReconstructionPath.actual` derives the complete issued path from the actual encoded source call and initial `Ready`, source availability and replacement availability. `complete` also constructs that call. The path retains issued names; returned-table `Grows` alone does not supply them. The filter path adds no Mu. No observation equality follows just from path membership.

`CausalStoredMuGenerators.selected` recovers the exact retained first writer from the selected full record through `NativeMuGrowthWriters.from_empty`. Full candidate equality identifies its entry, body, depth and ordinary probe. It does not identify its source syntax with the current requester. `complete` constructs the two sequential calls and issued paths, replays both at one produced final reader, retains the selected cell and availability, and derives both generator memberships there. Its additional profile and physical Empty results come from causal readback. They do not compare guarded rows. The final `unfolding` also returns both actual epsilon memberships, warm ordinary replay, output availability and the selected final cell. These are projections of the same completed schedule.

The original reader and the produced final reader remain distinct. `unfolding` proves that the chosen left endpoint is an epsilon successor at each reader. It proves `Generated` at the final reader. It does not assert that every origin of this endpoint is equal across the readers. `ObservationReaderSupport.same_iff` needs a genuine epsilon- and child-closed warm support; `Keeps` alone is insufficient.

The existing finite writer theorem remains conditional. `PublicationFiniteRootReconstruction.BodyIH` demands full-depth actual execution comparison for literal Mu atoms and lower-depth ordered child comparison. `RetainedMuFiniteUnfoldEquation.finite` consumes this hypothesis. Its `all_depths` requires it at every depth. `WriterFiniteNodeAlignment.warm` discharges the node root using genuine strict original child alignment, including physical row survival. It does not prove general nested binder alignment.

The chronology APIs do not already prove the missing semantic step. `PublicationHeapInduction.path_heap` receives the publication obligation explicitly. `CausalHistoryProfiles.history_heap` discharges its root-profile instance. That predicate is state-independent. A reader-relative all-depth predicate cannot be substituted without a separate retention or universal-reader proof. An earlier writer can have source syntax of a different size from the requester. The size of a smart-unused returned raw value also does not bound the selected candidate or its stored body.

The current proofs establish concrete causal schedules and root-profile/Empty agreement. They do not establish all-depth guarded source comparison, original-query-operand ancestry, general traversal termination, or Haskell correspondence. This review found no material issue in the selected writer chain.
